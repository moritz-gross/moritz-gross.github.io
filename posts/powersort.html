Powersort

This article is a work in progress. Details and examples may change.

Powersort is a modern sorting algorithm (proposed 2018) that keeps the same worst-case guarantees of previous algorithms like Quicksort and Mergesort, but works particularly fast on "easy" inputs. This leads us to first define what we exactly mean by easy and hard, and how we can exploit these ideas reliably without spending much computation on them.

Even though all following thoughts can be applied to any comparable type of object (e.g. strings), we use integers in the range from 1 to 10 for easy visualization.
